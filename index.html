Everything your crew needs, at a glance.

Six things Koolabo replaces for your department, all in one view.

Multiple Projects

Completely separated. One exception.

Messages and files never cross between projects — different crews belong to different projects, and switching between them is instant and context-safe. Calendars are the one exception: an optional All Projects view lets you see every project's schedule in one place.

Crew Communication

Replace texts and emails. One place everyone checks.

Department heads publish to crew channels, so decisions don't get buried in side texts and group chats.

Files

Everyone sees the latest version. No lost links.

Upload lighting plots, shot lists, breakdowns, references, whatever your department needs. Replace them anytime. Crew always knows they have the current file.

Location Scouting

Walk the location before you get there. 360° virtual scouts.

Upload 360° photos from location scouts and let every department head explore the space on their own time. Plan lighting setups, camera positions, set dressing and more.

Department Calendar

Not the AD schedule. Your department's plan.

Coordinate prep days, gear returns, crew availability, and equipment checkouts. Department heads stay on top of their own domain without the noise of the full production schedule.

International Projects

Built-in translation.

Post in English. Your crew reads it in Spanish, French, German, Arabic, or Mandarin, automatically. International crews stay on the same page without extra effort.
