Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Koolabo ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Koolabo application and website. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, and password when you register
  • Profile information: job title and profile photo if you choose to provide them
  • Usage data: messages, files, and project content you create within the app
  • Device information: device type, operating system, and push notification tokens for delivering notifications
  • Location: if you allow it, your device's approximate or precise location, used only to show weather and sunrise and sunset on your dashboard when your schedule has no shoot address for the day. It is sent with the request, is not stored in your profile, and is not used for advertising or tracking. You can turn it off at any time in your device settings
  • Payment information: processed securely by Stripe; we do not store credit card numbers

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the Koolabo service
  • To show local weather and daylight times for your shoot day
  • To send push notifications and email digests about project activity
  • To process subscription payments
  • To improve the app based on usage patterns
  • To respond to support requests

File Storage

Files uploaded to Koolabo are stored securely using Cloudflare R2 cloud storage. Files are only accessible to members of the project they were uploaded to.

AI Features

Some Koolabo features use artificial intelligence: reading a call sheet or schedule you upload, the Ask assistant, suggested jobs and crew lists, and translating chat messages. When you use one of these features, we send the content you chose (for example the call sheet file, your question, or the message to translate) to Google's Gemini and Cloud Translation services, together with limited context about the project you are working in, so the feature can respond.

  • We use Google's paid services. Under Google's terms for paid services, Google does not use this content to train or improve its models. Google may process and temporarily store it to provide the service and keep it safe.
  • AI only suggests. Nothing is created or changed in your account until you review and confirm it.
  • Call sheets and schedules can contain other people's names, phone numbers, and email addresses. Only upload documents you are allowed to share. When you confirm a suggested job, named crew are saved to your private People list. Koolabo does not send them invitations or messages unless you choose to invite them.
  • Please do not upload sensitive personal information you do not want processed by these services.

Third-Party Services

Koolabo uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase (database and authentication)
  • Cloudflare R2 (file storage)
  • Stripe (payment processing)
  • Resend (transactional email)
  • Google (Gemini AI and Cloud Translation for the AI features above; Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notifications; Google sign-in)
  • Apple (Sign in with Apple and in-app purchases)
  • RevenueCat (in-app subscription management)
  • Railway (application hosting)
  • Open-Meteo (weather and sunrise/sunset forecasts, using coordinates only)
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim (turning a schedule's shoot address into map coordinates)

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and data by contacting us at support@koolabo.com.

Children's Privacy

Koolabo is for people 18 and older and is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a person under 18 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. We will notify users of significant changes via email or in-app notice.
