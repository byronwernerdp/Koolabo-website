Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 12, 2026

Koolabo ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Koolabo application and website. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, and password when you register
  • Profile information: job title and profile photo if you choose to provide them
  • Usage data: messages, files, and project content you create within the app
  • Device information: device type, operating system, and push notification tokens for delivering notifications
  • Payment information: processed securely by Stripe; we do not store credit card numbers

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the Koolabo service
  • To send push notifications and email digests about project activity
  • To process subscription payments
  • To improve the app based on usage patterns
  • To respond to support requests

File Storage

Files uploaded to Koolabo are stored securely using Cloudflare R2 cloud storage. Files are only accessible to members of the project they were uploaded to.

Third-Party Services

Koolabo uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase (database and authentication)
  • Cloudflare R2 (file storage)
  • Stripe (payment processing)
  • Resend (transactional email)
  • Google (translation and AI features)

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and data by contacting us at support@koolabo.com.

Children's Privacy

Koolabo is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. We will notify users of significant changes via email or in-app notice.

Contact

Questions? Email us at support@koolabo.com.
